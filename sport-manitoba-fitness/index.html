The Training Space

A 160m indoor three-lane track ringed by skyline windows, plus Woodway, Jacobs Ladder, Atlantis, TRX and kettlebells.

Classes & Programs

Group classes, seasonal programs and workshops — including the 21-Day Fitness Challenge each season.

Personal & Small Group Training

One-to-one coaching, or small group training with a custom plan refreshed every three months and progress assessments.

More than a gym

Expert support for the health and fitness goals of all members and guests.

The Fitness Centre App

Animated workouts, class and program registration, and custom programs from your trainer.

Free Equipment Orientations

New to the space? We offer free-of-charge equipment orientations — email us to book.

Membership With a Mission

Revenue from your membership goes toward developing Manitoba's amateur sport system and strengthening communities.
