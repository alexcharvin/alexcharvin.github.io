Services & Treatments

Advanced. Unconventional. Fast Treatments.

Specialised care across Conservative Dentistry, Orthodontics, Implantology, Oral Surgery, Periodontics, Pedodontics and Oral Pathology.

Dental Implants

A permanent fix for missing teeth — an artificial root topped with a crown. Natural look, full function.

Invisalign

Clear aligners that straighten teeth without metal braces. Nearly invisible, comfortable, easy to wear.

Smile Makeover

Whitening, veneers, bonding and orthodontics in one plan, tailored to your smile.

Root Canal Treatment

Virtually painless root canal care, finished with a crown or filling so the tooth works normally.

Laser Assisted Dentistry

Lasers for decay, gum reshaping and whitening — precise treatment, minimal discomfort.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

One complete plan — crowns, bridges, orthodontics and cosmetic work — restoring health, function and looks.

Crowns, Onlays & Veneers

For decay, fractures, gaps and discolouration — restoring shape, strength and appearance.

Teeth Whitening

In-office whitening for instant results, plus supervised take-home kits.

Orthodontic Treatment

Straighter teeth, a better bite, better oral health — for all ages.

Periodontal & Gum Care

Prevention and treatment of gum disease, protecting the tissue and bone that hold your teeth.

Removable Dentures

Comfortable, natural-looking dentures — partial or complete. Eat and speak with ease.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Dentures anchored on implants — a stable fit, more confidence, helps preserve jawbone.
