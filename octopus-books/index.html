On our shelves

A progressive bookstore with range — poetry, graphic novels and puzzles & games too.

Fiction & Literature

New releases and backlist gems, chosen by booksellers who read — plus a dedicated section for local Ottawa authors.

Ideas That Matter

Politics, history, philosophy and social justice — and one of the city's best shelves of Indigenous writers.

Children's Books

A well-loved kids' section, stacked for young readers of every age — picture books to young adult.

How to shop with us

Three ways to be part of the store.

1

Browse in the store

Slow down among the shelves at 116 Third Avenue — the kind of shop that makes you want to stay a while.

2

Order for pickup or delivery

3
