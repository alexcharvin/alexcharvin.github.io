What we do

Honest, reliable dentistry Perth families have trusted for over 40 years.

Preventative Dentistry

Prevention is better than a cure — regular six-monthly check-ups and hygiene visits keep teeth and gums healthy.

Children's & Family Care

A home-like environment for the whole family, including children — post-treatment guidance so you can care for your smile at home.

Care for Anxious Patients

Decades of experience with nervous patients, with nitrous oxide and sedation facilities to help you relax.

The HBA way

A close-knit team of long-term staff, from your first phone call onward.

Check-ups & Hygiene

Thorough examinations and hygienist care that can prevent gum disease before it starts — early detection is everything.

Honest Advice, Always

We will never sell unnecessary treatments. We discuss all your options and encourage you to make your own choice.

Restorative Care

Safe, hygienic, high-quality treatments tailored to your family — from fillings to complete restorative work.
